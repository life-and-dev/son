# Is Jesus God?

## Key Terms

- **Nicene Trinitarian** wording defines 1 divine essence in 3 distinct persons meaning God became a man.
- **Unitarians** understand God as Father alone, Jesus as fully human Messiah, and Spirit as God's active power and presence.
