# Did Jesus Claim To Be Divine?

Jesus never directly claimed to be God himself. The divinity of Jesus needs to be interpreted from his "indirect" claims listed below.

* [Jesus claims to be one with the Father](claims/1-with-father.md)
* [Jesus claims to forgave sin against God](claim/forgiveness.md)
* [Jesus claims to be glorified](claims/glory.md)
* [Jesus claims to be in His disciples](claims/in-god.md)
* [Jesus claims to be the life](claims/life.md)
* [Jesus claims to be the light](claims/light.md)
* [Jesus claims that you receive God when you receive him](claims/receive-god.md)
* [Jesus claims to be the Good Shepherd](claims/shepherd.md)
* [Jesus claims the throne](claims/throne.md)
* [Jesus claims to his yoke is easy](claims/yoke.md)
* [Jesus claims to come from God](https://eternal.family.net.za/god/son/essence/as-god/claims/come-from-god)

Some would object to say that Jesus could not make direct claims, *because he knew the people would not be able to handle this new revelation that a god could become a man*. However in the Greco-Roman world it was common belief that gods like Apollo could take the form of a human being. Likewise the idea that a human could become a god was also common. When Paul healed someone, the Greeks thought that Paul and Barnabas were pagan gods who descended to them (​Acts 14:11–13).

This begs the question:

* Why did Jesus never said he was God?
* If Jesus did said that he was God, why didn't the Gospel authors recorded these statements?

Some would argue [the Trinity](../trinity.md) is to complex to explain to *simple* Jews. However, Jesus always used parables to explain complex subjects. Why are there no analogies recorded regarding [the Trinity](../trinity.md)? For example Jesus could have illustated *God is like water, ice and steam* if he was a [Modalist](../modalism.md) or God is like an egg (shell, white and yolk) if he was a [Partialist](../partialism.md).
