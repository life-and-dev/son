# Jesus was Sinless

The presumption is that *only God can be sinless*. Yet the bible says Jesus was sinless.

> For our sake he made him to be sin who **knew no sin**, so that in him we might become the righteousness of God. — 2 Corinthians 5:21 (ESV)

Therefore, some argue that Jesus had to be divine in order to be a sinless sacrifice that could atone for our sin.

## Rebuttal

Jesus' sinlessness does not by itself establish that he is divine. The following examples distinguish being without sin from being God:

1. God's angels: "In the presence of God and of Christ Jesus" are God's "elect angels" (1 Timothy 5:21). The angels who did sin, were cast out of Heaven, known as fallen angels. If only God can be sinless, then it means God's own angels are still committing sin (disobeying God).
2. Adam before he sinned: "God saw everything that He had made and behold, was very good" (Genesis 1:31). Adam was made without sin, without being divine. Adam choose to sin (Genesis 3), but there was a period where Adam too was sinless.
3. Resurrected Righteous: God will not tolerate sin. Revelation 21:27 is clear. Those who will be resurrected will be sinless without being gods.

> [!NOTE]
> One speculation is that the virgin birth enabled Jesus to be sinless because he did not inherit sin from Adam like other children, having been conceived through the Holy Spirit. Scripture does not explicitly give this as the reason for the virgin birth or say that it explains Jesus' sinlessness.

Obedience (sinlessness) is not exclusive to the Almighty God.
