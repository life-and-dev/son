# 1 John 5:20 (The True God)

> And we know **that the Son of God has come** and has given us understanding, **so that we may know him who is true**; and **we are in him who is true**, in his Son Jesus Christ. **He is the true God** and eternal life. — 1 John 5:20 (ESV)

Out of context and the way it is translated, it seems like "the Son of God" is "the true God".

This raises an immediate **paradox within the same sentence**, showing that *God is His own Son*. [Trinitarians](../../trinity.md) would explain this fits their doctrine because of their shared essence, meaning *"the Son of God" is also "the God the Father"*, yet distinct persons with a Father-son relationship. This is all based on [philosophy developed many centuries](../../trinity/development.md) later. Therefore no Bible scripture will support this reasoning. If Jesus and God the Father had this special relationship, then the Bible authors should not have used common "Father" and "Son" terminology to describe it. It all human languages no "father" and "son" and can be the same individual.

## Textual Variation

The manuscript [A(02) reads “so that we may know the true **God**”](https://corebible.app/2PE/1?verseNumber=%2228%22&translation=AICNT) which clarify that "who is true" is not "the Son of God" in this context.

## Context

In John's life-time there was an ancient belief that [Christ was divine](../../divine.md) but *not genuinely human*, called **Docetism**. The term comes from the Greek word dokeō (δοκέω), meaning “to seem” or “to appear.” Docetism taught that *Jesus only appeared to have a physical human body, suffer, and die*, rather than experiencing these things as a genuine human being. This belief appeared in various forms in early Christianity, particularly during the second century, although related ideas may have existed earlier. This is evident from scriptures like:

> What was from the beginning, what **we have heard**, what **we have seen** with our eyes, what **we beheld and touched** with our hands, concerning [the Word of Life](../../name/word.md)—and **the life was manifested**, and we have seen and bear witness and proclaim to you [the eternal life](https://kingdom.ofgod.info/life), which was with the Father and was manifested to us— what we have **seen** and **heard** we proclaim to you also, so that you may also have fellowship with us; and indeed our fellowship is with the Father, and with His Son Jesus Christ. — 1 John 1:1-3 (LSB)

John repeatedly mention that they physically heard, seen and touched Jesus Christ. John want to make sure his audience clearly understood that Jesus was [genuinely human](../../human.md).

During the book John identifies all sorts of deceptions and show [how to identify the spirit the Antichrist](../../shema/concerns/antichrist.md). For example:

> By this you know the Spirit of God: every spirit that **confesses that Jesus Christ** has **come in the flesh** is from God, and every spirit that does **not confess Jesus** is not from God. This is the spirit of the **antichrist**, which you heard was coming and now is in the world already. — 1 John 4:2-3 (ESV)

> [!TIP]
> ["Christ" means *anointed*](https://kingdom.ofgod.info/christ), so "*anti*christ" would mean *not annointed*.

> [!TIP]
> "Come in flesh" means to be born as a human (John 1:14).

This scripture refutes Docetism and Christology (Jesus is God), because it shows Christ was anointed by God and that he was born as a human.

John even concludes his book with the warning:

> Little children, **guard yourselves from idols**. — 1 John 5:21 (LSB)
