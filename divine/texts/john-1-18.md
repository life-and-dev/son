# John 1:18 (Son Who Is Himself God)

Some modern English bibles read:

> “No one has ever seen God, but the one and only **Son, who is himself God** and is in closest relationship with the Father, has made him known.” — John 1:18 (NIV)

and

> No one has ever seen God; **God the only Son**, who is at the Father’s side, he has made him known. — John 1:18 (ESV)

and

> No one has seen God at any time; **the only begotten God** who is in the bosom of the Father, He has explained Him. — John 1:18 (NA95), but also BLB & LSB

This is to support the [Trinitarian doctrine](../../trinity.md) that *the Son is God*.

However, the KJV bible read:

> No man hath seen God at any time; **the only begotten Son**, which is in the bosom of the Father, he hath declared him. — John 1:18 (KJV), but also NKJV

- The older editions render "Son": RP Byzantine 2005, Greek Orthodox, Tischendorf 8th Edition, Scriver 1894, Stephanus 1550
- The newer editions render "God": Nestle 1904, Westcott & Hort 1881, NA27

This was a highly debated verse during the development of the Trinity. [Many early church fathers quoted this verse](https://youtu.be/inKyhLUpbk4?si=J4BWvSK22zG6_f8m) as it read during their lifetime. For example **Irenaeus (180 AD)** (Against Heresies, Book III, Chapter 11), **Clement of Alexandria (180 AD)** (Stromateis 1:26 and Rich Man part 37) and **Turtillian (200 AD)** (Against Praxeus 15:6) all quoted the scripture as "only begotten **Son**".

Even the author John himself, speaks of Jesus as:

> For God so loved the world that He gave His **only begotten Son**, that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life. — John 3:16 (NKJV)

John would not have contradicted himself.
