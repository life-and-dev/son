# John 12:41 (Isaiah Saw ... His Hlory)

> Though he *[Jesus]* had done so many signs before them, **they still did not believe *in* him**, so that the word spoken by the prophet Isaiah might be fulfilled:
>
>> “LORD, who has believed what he heard from us, and to whom has the arm of the LORD been revealed?”
>
> Therefore they could not believe. For again Isaiah said,
>
>> “He has blinded their eyes and hardened their heart, lest they see with their eyes, and understand with their heart, and turn, and I would heal them.”
>
> **Isaiah said these things because he saw His glory and spoke of Him.**
>
> Nevertheless, many even of **the authorities believed in him**, but for fear of the Pharisees they did not confess it, so that they would not be put out of the synagogue; — John 12:37-42 (ESV)

In many modern English translations like the ESV, it is clear that the "him" refers to Jesus in verse 37 and 42. Therefore, it is logical to conclude that the "Him" of John 12:41 may refer to Jesus too.

The scriptures that has been quoted in Isaiah refers to [Yahweh](https://kingdom.ofgod.info/name#yhwh) (the LORD).

> Who has believed our report?  
> And to whom has the arm of **Yahweh** been revealed? — Isaiah 53:1 (LSB)

and

> And one called out to another and said,
>
> “Holy, Holy, Holy, is **Yahweh** of hosts; The whole earth is full of **His glory**.”
>
> ...
>
> Render the hearts of this people insensitive, their ears dull, and their eyes dim. Lest they see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their hearts, and return and be healed.”
>
> — Isaiah 6:3,10 (LSB)

Therefore, they conclude that *Jesus is Yahweh* according to John and Yahweh is the Holy Almighty God, "the LORD of Hosts".

However, John 12:41 contains an important textual variation:

> Some manuscripts read “the glory of his God.” D(05) Latin(d)
> Other manuscripts including the f13 family read “the glory of God.”
>
> — [AICNT](https://corebible.app/JHN/12?verseNumber=%2244%22&translation=AICNT)

In addition the next verses confirm these manuscripts:

> ... for they loved the glory that comes from man more than **the glory that comes from God**. And Jesus cried out and said, “Whoever believes *in* me, **believes not in me but in Him Who sent me**. — John 12:43-44 (ESV)

John explicitly refered to "the glory that come from God". This is a different glory than the glory of Isaiah 6:3. If Jesus was Yahweh, why would his glory come from another God?

In addition, John explains that "believing Jesus" means **to belief in the God Who sent him**. John is not correcting himself. This is how [biblical agency](../../shema/agency.md) works:

Jesus acts as God's delegated agent demonstrating His character and authority (John 12:45).

> For I have **not spoken on my own authority, but the Father** Who **sent me** has Himself given me a commandment⁠—**what to say and what to speak**. And I know that His commandment is eternal life. **What I say, therefore, I say as the Father has told me.**” — John 12:49-50 (ESV)
