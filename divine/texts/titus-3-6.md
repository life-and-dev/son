# Titus 3:6 (Jesus Christ Our God)

The earliest manuscripts and most modern English bible Translation render:

> ... through Jesus Christ **our Savior**... — Titus 3:6 (ESV)

Yet, some late Latin manuscripts render:

> ... through Jesus Christ **our *God and* Saviour**...

This changes he meaning of the text significantly and most scholars agree it is an error.

Manuscripts in favour of the shorter "our Savior": Nestle 1904, Westcott & Hort 1881, NA27/UBS4, Greek Orthodox, Tischendorf 8th Edition, Scriveners Textus Receptive 1894, Stephanus Textus Receptive 1550.
