# Jesus Claims to Be the Life

## Context and Response

At Lazarus’ tomb, Jesus says, “I am the resurrection and the life,” then prays to the Father before Lazarus is raised so that the crowd may believe that the Father sent him (John 11:25-27,41-42). The immediate event displays Jesus’ authority over death.

The strongest Trinitarian reading understands “the life” as intrinsic divine life. John’s Gospel gives the Son a uniquely life-giving role.

John also states its source: the Father granted the Son to have life in himself and gave him authority to execute judgement (John 5:26-27). God raised Jesus from the dead (Acts 2:24,32). Jesus gives resurrection life as the Father’s authorised Messiah and mediator. Bestowed life-giving authority establishes his unique office, not shared essence with the one God.
