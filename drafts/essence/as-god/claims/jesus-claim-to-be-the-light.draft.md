# Jesus Claims to Be the Light

## Context and Response

In John 8:12, Jesus says, “I am the light of the world,” immediately after controversy in the temple and before explaining that the Father sent him and bears witness to him (John 8:14-18). The image recalls YHVH as light and salvation (Psalm 27:1).

The strongest Trinitarian reading takes that echo, together with John’s elevated language about Jesus, as a claim to divine identity. Jesus does claim a unique, world-wide role.

Scripture also gives the servant of YHVH a light-to-the-nations mission (Isaiah 42:6; 49:6). John’s context identifies Jesus as the sent one whose testimony is confirmed by the Father. Jesus brings God’s saving revelation and leads people from darkness as the commissioned Messiah. That representative role is extraordinary, but it does not by itself mean that he shares YHVH’s essence.
