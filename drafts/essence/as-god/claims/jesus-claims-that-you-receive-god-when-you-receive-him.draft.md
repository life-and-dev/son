# Receiving God When Receiving Jesus

## Context and Response

In Matthew 10:40, Jesus tells the apostles, “Whoever receives you receives me, and whoever receives me receives him who sent me.” The immediate context is their mission as his authorised representatives (Matthew 10:5-20).

The strongest Trinitarian reading reasons that receiving Jesus is receiving God because Jesus shares God’s identity. The saying does bind a person’s response to Jesus inseparably to a response to the Father.

Its three-part chain also explains how this works: receiving an apostle counts as receiving Jesus, yet no apostle shares Jesus’ essence. John 13:20 repeats the same agency principle for those Jesus sends. In biblical representation, honouring an authorised messenger honours the sender. Jesus uniquely represents the Father as His sent Son, but representation and mission do not establish shared essence.
