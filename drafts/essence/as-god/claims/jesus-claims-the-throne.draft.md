# Jesus Claims the Throne

## Context and Response

Revelation 3:21 says that Jesus conquered and sat down with his Father on His throne. The verse follows Jesus’ promise that a conquering believer will sit with him on his throne.

The strongest Trinitarian reading sees Jesus sharing the Father’s throne as shared divine sovereignty. Revelation’s worship and royal imagery make this a weighty claim.

The verse itself presents throne-sharing as granted participation, since victorious believers also receive a place on Jesus’ throne without becoming God in essence. Elsewhere, the Father seats the risen Jesus at His right hand (Psalm 110:1; Acts 2:34-36), and Revelation distinguishes “God and the Lamb” (Revelation 22:1-3). Jesus reigns as God’s exalted Messiah; shared rule by appointment does not make the recipient identical with YHVH.
