# Jesus’ Claims

## Context and Response

The linked claim pages consider distinct questions: Jesus’ unity with the Father, mutual indwelling, the Son of Man, glory, his yoke, and the authority to judge, forgive, give life, and receive honour. Their shared conclusion is not that these claims are ordinary. Each presents Jesus as God’s uniquely authorised Messiah and representative.

The strongest Trinitarian case combines their exceptional scope and concludes that Jesus bears divine identity. That case takes seriously the authority given in John 5:19-27 and the universal authority Jesus says has been given to him in Matthew 28:18.

The linked contexts repeatedly distinguish the Father as giver from Jesus as recipient and agent. God raises and exalts Jesus (Acts 2:32-36), and Jesus calls the Father “the only true God” while identifying himself as the one sent by him (John 17:3). The claims therefore support Jesus’ unique, exalted Messiahship without by themselves erasing the distinction between YHVH and his human Son.
