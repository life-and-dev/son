# Did Jesus Already Come From Heaven?

John’s language about heaven, descent, and sending raises a question about Jesus’ origin. The context shows how heavenly source and God-given authority relate to the human Messiah.

## John’s Descent Language

> “No one has ascended into heaven except he who descended from heaven, the Son of Man.” — John 3:13 (ESV)

The strongest Trinitarian reading understands descent literally: an eternal Son came from heaven. In John 3, however, descent supports Jesus’ authority to reveal heavenly things. John says that God sent him, gave him the Spirit without measure, and gave all things into his hand (John 3:34-35). John the Baptist similarly says that a person receives only what is given from heaven (John 3:27).

This language can identify divine source and commission without requiring a personal journey before birth. The longer phrase “who is in heaven” is absent from important early manuscripts and cannot establish simultaneous heavenly and earthly existence. A two-natures appeal goes beyond John’s stated distinction. The Father sends and authorises his human Son.
