# Did Jesus Come Forth From God?

Jesus said that he came from the Father and returned to the Father. The birth accounts and John’s mission language clarify what that claim does and does not state.

## Origin and Mission

> “I came from the Father and have come into the world, and now I am leaving the world and going to the Father.” — John 16:28 (ESV)

The strongest Trinitarian reading treats this as a personal departure from a pre-human life with the Father. John also explains Jesus’ origin through sending: “I did not come of my own accord, but he sent me” (John 8:42). Coming from God identifies source, authority, and agency.

The conception accounts identify how the Son came into the world. God’s Holy Spirit and power brought about the conception in Mary (Matthew 1:18,20; Luke 1:35). The Spirit is God’s power, not a biological father. A two-natures appeal adds an eternal life behind John’s words, whereas these texts describe the Father’s initiative and the obedience of his human Messiah.
