## Did Jesus See Satan Fall From Heaven?

Jesus’ saying about Satan falling from heaven is often read as a memory of a pre-human life. Its mission setting explains the image and the authority behind it.

### Luke 10:18

> “I saw Satan fall like lightning from heaven.” — Luke 10:18 (ESV)

The strongest Trinitarian reading takes this as a report from a pre-human heavenly life. The seventy-two have just returned, rejoicing that demons submit in Jesus’ name (Luke 10:17). Jesus answers by describing Satan’s fall and giving them authority over the enemy (Luke 10:18-19).

The saying depicts Satan’s defeat as Jesus’ God-given mission advances. Prophetic speech can describe a future or unfolding defeat as seen and certain, as Isaiah depicts Babylon’s fall from heaven (Isaiah 14:12-15). A two-natures appeal is not supplied by Luke. The passage concerns authority God gives through his human Messiah.
