### Is Jesus the Sabbath?

> “For the Son of Man is lord of the Sabbath.” — Matthew 12:8 (ESV)

No. Scripture calls Jesus Lord of the Sabbath, but it does not identify him as the Sabbath.

The strongest Trinitarian reading may take Jesus’ lordship as evidence that he is the Sabbath’s divine giver. In context, Jesus defends his disciples by citing David, priests, mercy, and the temple (Matthew 12:3-7). He identifies himself as the Son of Man with authority in that dispute.

God established the Sabbath at creation and gave it to Israel (Genesis 2:2-3; Exodus 20:8-11). Jesus’ authority is received: God made him both Lord and Christ after raising him (Acts 2:36). A two-natures appeal explains this authority by an eternal divine nature, but Matthew does not state that explanation. The passage fits God’s authorised human Messiah.
