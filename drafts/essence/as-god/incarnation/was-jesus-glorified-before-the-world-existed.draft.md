## Was Jesus Glorified Before the World Existed?

John 17 connects Jesus’ requested glory with the time before the world existed. Biblical language about God’s foreknown purpose and promised glory supplies the context.

### John 17:5

> “Glorify me in your own presence with the glory that I had with you before the world existed.” — John 17:5 (ESV)

The strongest Trinitarian reading takes this as direct evidence that Jesus personally shared divine glory before creation. In the same prayer, Jesus says believers were given to him before the world existed and speaks of glory given to them (John 17:2,22,24).

Believers were chosen in Christ before the foundation of the world, though they did not personally live then (Ephesians 1:4). This is foreordination, or prophetic ideal existence: a reality secure in God’s purpose before fulfilment. Jesus asks for glory promised to God’s obedient Messiah. A two-natures appeal makes a divine nature personally possess eternal glory, but John calls the Father the only true God and Jesus the one sent by him (John 17:3).
