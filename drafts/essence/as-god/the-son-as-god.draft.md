The strongest Trinitarian case observes that Jesus has divine-seeming titles, works, honor, and authority. The following themes are important and deserve careful reading:

* [He is eternal](/son/essence/as-god/eternal.md) (Revelation 1:17-18)
* [He has power over death](/son/essence/as-god/jesus-was-resurrected.md) (John 5:21, Revelation 1:17-18)
* [He has power to heal](/son/essence/as-god/jesus-did-miracles.md) (John 4:46-54, 5:8-9)
* [He has authority to judge or forgive sin](/son/essence/as-god/jesus-forgave-sin-against-god.md) (Matthew 10:32-33, John 5:22; Mark 2:10)
* [He is the foundation ("the rock")](/son/essence/as-god/incarnation/jesus-is-the-rock.md) (1 Corinthians 10:4)
* [He will return to judge the world](/son/essence/as-god/prophecies/jesus-prophecies.md) (Matthew 16:27-28; Revelation 22:1-5)
* He is the trustworthy witness ("truth" or ["light"](/kingdom/light.md)) (John 1:5, 3:19)
* He rules over our world (Matthew 28:18; Psalm 2:8-12; Isaiah 42:1-4; Hebrews 2:7-8; Philippians 2:10-11)
* He looks after his people (John 10:14)

The immediate Unitarian answer is not that these texts diminish Christ. God can give his human Messiah authority, life, judgment, glory, and a universal kingdom. Jesus repeatedly distinguishes himself from the one God: the Father is "the only true God," while Jesus is the Christ sent by him (John 17:3). The Father is "the God of our Lord Jesus Christ" (Ephesians 1:17).

[God the Father](/ofgod/father/god-the-father.md) alone is the one God of Israel and has these attributes in the underived sense:

* The Father is eternal. (Isaiah 44:6; Isaiah 48:12; Exodus 3:14-15)
* The Father also has power over death (Acts 2:24)
* The Father also has power to heal (Exodus 15:26)
* The Father also has the authority to judge or forgive sin (Genesis 18:25, Exodus 34:7; Numbers 14:17, Matthew 6:14-15)
* The Father is called "the rock" (Deuteronomy 32:4; Psalm 18:2; 1 Samuel 2:2; 2 Samuel 22:47)
* The Father judges the world through the man he appointed (Acts 17:31).
* The Father is the source of truth or ["light"](/kingdom/light.md) (Psalm 27:1; 1 John 1:5-7)
* The Father rules over all (1 Corinthians 15:24-28).
* The Father cares for his people (Matthew 6:26; Matthew 7:11; James 1:17).

The [Shema](/son/shema.md) teaches that there is one God (Deuteronomy 6:4). Jesus affirms it and identifies that one as God, while calling himself God's Christ (Mark 12:29; John 17:3).

[Christians](/church/terms/christian.md) commonly resolve this distinction through [Trinity doctrine](/son/trinity.md): Father, Son, and Holy Spirit are one God. The immediate and canonical Unitarian answer instead receives the distinctions as given: YHVH is the Father alone; Jesus is his human Son and Christ; and the Holy Spirit is God's Spirit and power (John 17:3; Acts 2:22; Acts 1:8). The word ["God" has many different meanings](/word/terms/god.md), and Scripture can apply *god* representatively to human rulers (Psalm 82:6; John 10:34-36). Such titles never erase the difference between YHVH and his agents.

In addition, to proof that Jesus is God, some also belief:

* [Jesus made the world](/son/son-as-god/creator.md)
* [Jesus is God who reincarnated](/son/essence/as-god/incarnation/incarnation-of-jesus.md)

But [the Bible](/word/drafts/word-the-bible.md) clearly distinguishes [YHVH (the LORD), the Almighty God and Father](/ofgod/father/god-the-father.md) from Jesus. Jesus is God's obedient, exalted agent; his honor, worship, and delegated rule ultimately glorify the Father (Philippians 2:9-11; 1 Corinthians 15:27-28). The following identify the Father as the one Almighty God:

* [There can only be one God](/son/shema.md) (Deuteronomy 6:4; Mark 12:29; Ephesians 4:6)
* [God would refer to himself as God](/son/son-of-man/denies-being-god.md) (Luke 4:17-20; Isaiah 61:1-2)
* [God does not reincarnate](/son/son-of-man/human.md) (Numbers 23:19; 1 Kings 8:27; Psalm 90:2)
* [God requires worship](/son/son-of-man/serve-god.md) (1 Samuel 7:3-4; Matthew 4:10; Luke 4:8)
* [God has highest authority](/son/son-of-man/serve-god.md) (John 7:16; John 14:24; Psalm 47:2)
* [God does not have limitations](/son/son-of-man/limitations.md) (Job 11:7; Luke 2:52)
* [God is immortal](/son/son-of-man/limitations.md#jesus-died) (Romans 1:22-23; 1 Timothy 1:17)
* [God is omnipresent](/son/son-of-man/limitations.md#jesus-is-not-omnipresent) (Psalm 139:7-10; Ephesians 4:6)
* [God cannot be seen](/son/son-of-man/limitations.md#jesus-could-be-seen) (Exodus 33:20)
