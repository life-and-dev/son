# The Purpose of Jesus Christ

Why did Jesus Christ come into the world? While many see him as a moral teacher
or a historical figure, the Bible presents a multifaceted purpose for his life
and ministry. Exploring these reasons reveals a deeper understanding of his
identity and his mission to reconcile humanity with the Creator.

Because humanity had [sinned](../essence/as-god/jesus-forgave-sin-against-god.md),
sinners are condemned to [death](../nature.md#authority-over-death).
However, Jesus loved humanity enough to reconcile humanity with the Father at
[the crucifixion](../crucifixion/the-crucifixion.md).

> For the [Son of Man](../son-of-man.md) came to seek and to save the lost. —
> Luke 19:10 (ESV)

Because...

> As [the Father](../nature.md#the-father) has loved me, so have I loved you.
> Abide in my love. — John 15:9 (ESV)

Therefore...

> Jesus said to him, "I am [the way](#the-way), and [the truth](#the-truth), and
> [the life](#the-life). No one comes to the Father except through me." — John
> 14:6 (ESV)

## The Way

Jesus was not intended to be the final destination. Instead, he serves as "the
way" to God. As a mediator, he acts as a bridge between God and humanity,
facilitating reconciliation and access to the Father.

> This is good, and it is pleasing in the sight of God our Savior, who desires
> all people to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth. For there is
> [one God](../shema.md), and there is one mediator between God and men, the man
> [Christ](../christ/christ.md) Jesus, who gave himself as a ransom for all,
> which is the testimony given at the proper time. — 1 Timothy 2:3-6 (ESV)

and

> For through him we both have access in one Spirit to the Father. So then you
> are no longer strangers and aliens, but you are fellow citizens with the
> saints and members of the household of God. — Ephesians 2:18-19 (ESV)

Peter said:

> For Christ also suffered once for sins, the righteous for the unrighteous,
> that he might bring us to God, being put to death in the flesh but made alive
> in the spirit. — 1 Peter 3:18 (ESV)

Some may question why Jesus is the only way to God. The scriptural argument is
that because Jesus is the only one who lived a sinless life and offered himself
as a perfect sacrifice, he alone can satisfy the requirements of divine justice
and bridge the gap caused by sin.

## The Truth

### Teach the Kingdom of God

> And when it was day, he departed and went into a desolate place. And the
> people sought him and came to him, and would have kept him from leaving them,
> but he said to them, “I must preach the good news of the kingdom of God to the
> other towns as well; for I was sent for this purpose.” And he was preaching in
> the synagogues of Judea. — Luke 4:42-44 (ESV)

This was Nicodemus’s witness:

> Now there was a man of the Pharisees named Nicodemus, a ruler of the Jews.
> This man came to Jesus by night and said to him, “Rabbi, we know that you are
> a teacher come from God, for no one can do these signs that you do unless God
> is with him.” — John 3:1-2 (ESV)

This was also confirmed by Paul:

> To me, though I am the very least of all the saints, this grace was given, to
> preach to the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of
> [Christ](../christ/christ.md), and to bring to light for everyone what is the
> plan of [the mystery](../trinity.md#mystery) hidden for ages in God, who
> created all things, so that through the church the manifold wisdom of God
> might now be made known to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly places.
> This was according to the eternal purpose that he has realized in Christ Jesus
> our Lord, in whom we have boldness and access with confidence through our
> faith in him. — Ephesians 3:8-12 (ESV)

And the author of Hebrews:

> Long ago, at many times and in many ways, God spoke to our fathers by the
> prophets, but in these last days he has spoken to us by His Son, whom he
> appointed the heir of all things, through whom also he created the world. —
> Hebrews 1:1-2 (ESV)

### Bear Witness to the Truth

> Jesus answered, “My kingdom is not of this world. If my kingdom were of this
> world, my servants would have been fighting, that I might not be delivered
> over to the Jews. But my kingdom is not from the world.” Then Pilate said to
> him, “So you are a king?” Jesus answered, “You say that I am a king. For this
> purpose I was born and for this purpose I have come into the world to bear
> witness to the truth. Everyone who is of the truth listens to my voice.” —
> John 18:36-37 (ESV)

### To Fulfill Prophecies

Prophecies are ancient predictions found in the Hebrew Scriptures that foretell
the coming of the Messiah. Jesus claimed that his life and actions were the
direct fulfillment of these divine promises.

> Do not think that I have come to abolish the Law or the Prophets; I have not
> come to abolish them but to fulfill them. For truly, I say to you, until
> heaven and earth pass away, not an iota, not a dot, will pass from the Law
> until all is accomplished. — Matthew 5:17-18 (ESV)

and

> Then he said to them, “These are my words that I spoke to you while I was
> still with you, that everything written about me in the Law of Moses and the
> Prophets and the Psalms must be fulfilled.” Then he opened their minds to
> understand the Scriptures, and said to them, “Thus it is written, that the
> Christ should suffer and on the third day rise from the dead, and that
> repentance for the forgiveness of sins should be proclaimed in his
> [name](../nature.md#jesus-name) to all nations, beginning from Jerusalem. —
> Luke 24:44-47 (ESV)

## The Life

> your wife, for that which is conceived in her is from the Holy Spirit. 21 She
> will bear a son, and you shall call his name Jesus, for he will save his
> people from their sins.” — Matthew 1:20-21 (ESV)

A few years later...

> Truly, truly, I say to you, unless a grain of wheat falls into the earth and

> eternal life. 26 If anyone serves me, he must follow me; and where I am, there
> will my servant be also. If anyone serves me, the Father will honor him. 27

> and heard it said that it had thundered. Others said, “[An angel](../son-as-
> angel.md) has spoken to him.” 30 Jesus answered, “This voice has come for your
> sake, not mine. 31 Now is the judgment of this world; now will the ruler of
> this world be cast out. 32 And I, when I am lifted up from the earth, will
> draw all people to myself.” 33 He said this to show by what kind of death he
> was going to die. 34 So the crowd answered him, “We have heard from the Law
> that the Christ remains forever. How can you say that [the Son of Man](../son-
> of-man.md) must be lifted up? [Who is this Son of Man?](../son-of-man.md)” 35
> So Jesus said to them, “[The light](../son-as-god/claims/light.md) is among

Good deeds cannot save:

> I do not nullify the grace of God, for if righteousness were through the law,
> then Christ died for no purpose. — Galatians 2:21 (ESV)

## Conclusion

The purpose of Jesus Christ encompasses his roles as [the Way](#the-way) to the
Father, the bearer of [the Truth](#the-truth), and the source of [the
Life](#the-life) for all who believe. Through his teachings, his fulfillment of
ancient prophecies, and his ultimate sacrifice, he achieved the eternal purpose
of reconciling humanity with God.
