# The Identity and Nature of the Son of God

The figure of Jesus Christ has captivated the hearts and minds of billions for
centuries. Known by many names and credited with extraordinary deeds, he
remains at the center of theological exploration. Who is this individual
described in ancient texts as both the son of a carpenter and the Son of God?
By examining the titles he bore and the life he led, we can begin to uncover
the complex nature of his identity and the profound impact of his mission.

## Biblical Titles and Names

Jesus is identified by numerous titles in the Bible. These names often reveal
specific aspects of his character or role.

* "[Wonderful Counselor](../essence/as-god/prophecies/isaiah-s-prophecies.draft.md)"
  (Isaiah 9:6)
* "[Mighty One](../essence/as-god/prophecies/isaiah-s-prophecies.draft.md)"
  (Isaiah 9:6)
* "[Abi-Ad](../essence/as-god/prophecies/isaiah-s-prophecies.draft.md)"
  (Isaiah 9:6), which is often translated as "Eternal Father" or "Father of
  Eternity"
* "[Prince of Peace](../essence/as-god/prophecies/isaiah-s-prophecies.draft.md)"
  (Isaiah 9:6)
* "The Vine" (John 15:1-9). This imagery connects to the vineyard of Israel
  mentioned in the Old Testament.
* "[Immanuel](../essence/as-god/prophecies/isaiah-s-prophecies.draft.md)"
  (Matthew 1:23; Isaiah 7:14), which means "[God](../nature.md) with us"
* "Jesus" (Mark 1:1)
* "[Holy One](../essence/as-god/jesus-is-holy.draft.md)" (Mark 1:24; Luke 1:35,
  4:34; John 6:69; Acts 2:27, 13:35 quoting Psalm 16:10)
* "[The Light](../son-as-god/claims/light.md)" (John 1:5, 3:19)
* "The Lamb of God" (John 1:29,36; 1 Corinthians 5:7; 1 Peter 1:19)
* "[The Bread](../son-as-god/claims/life.md)" (John 6:35,48-51)
* "The Door or Gate of the Sheep" (John 10:7)
* "The Good Shepherd" (John 10:14)
* "The Way, and the Truth, and the Life" (John 14:6; Acts 3:15)
* "[The Christ](../christ/christ.draft.md)" (Acts 2:36)
* "[The Firstborn](../firstborn.draft.md)" (Romans 8:29; Colossians 1:15,18;
  Hebrews 1:6; Revelation 1:5)
* "[The Cornerstone](../essence/as-god/incarnation/jesus-is-the-rock.draft.md)"
  (Ephesians 2:20; Zechariah 3:9; 1 Corinthians 10:4)
* "[King of Kings, Lord of Lords](../essence/as-god/lord.draft.md#king-of-kings)"
  (1 Timothy 6:15; Revelation 1:5, 17:14, 19:16). Historical figures such as
  Nebuchadnezzar (Daniel 2:37) and Artaxerxes (Ezra 7:12) also held the title
  of king of kings.
* "The Faithful Witness" (Revelation 1:5)
* "The First and the Last" (Revelation 1:8,17)
* "The Lion of Judah" (Revelation 5:5)

## Humanity and Emotions

The New Testament records that Jesus experienced life as a human being. He
displayed a wide range of emotions and faced social challenges.

* He expressed deep emotions (John 11:33-35).
* He was not always popular or well-received (Isaiah 53:2-3).
  * Critics called him a glutton and a drunkard. They also noted he was a
    friend of tax collectors and sinners (Matthew 11:19).
  * His own brothers did not believe in him at first and even mocked him
    (John 7:1-9).
  * People often judged him based on his humble appearance (John 7:24).
    * He did not wear the expensive or fine clothing typically associated
      with the wealthy (Matthew 11:8).
* Many recognized him as a prophet during his ministry (Matthew 11:9;
  Luke 1:76-79).
* He did not always adhere to the strict legalistic interpretations of the
  Pharisees (Matthew 12:1-2).
* Scripture indicates that he has a soul (Matthew 26:38; Mark 14:34).
* He is described as having his own
  [spirit](../drafts/the-spirit-of-jesus-christ.draft.md) (Matthew 27:50;
  Mark 2:8, 8:11-12; John 11:33, 13:21, 19:30).
* [He is human](../son-of-man/human.md) (Mark 15:39; John 8:40; Acts 2:22;
  Romans 5:15; 1 Timothy 2:5; Hebrews 10:12; Acts 17:30-31).
* God anointed him with [the Holy Spirit](../trinity/holy-spirit.md) and with
  power (Acts 10:38).
* [He was made in the image of God](../essence/what-is-jesus.draft.md)
  (Colossians 1:15).

## Divine Nature and Special Characteristics

While Jesus shared in the human experience, the Bible also highlights unique
qualities that distinguish him from others.

* [Numerous prophets spoke about him](../son-of-man/prophecies.md) centuries
  before his birth.
* [He is the Son of the Father](https://son.ofgod.info).
* [He was sent for a specific divine purpose](../drafts/purpose-of-jesus.draft.md).
* He stated that he is not from this world (John 8:23-24).
* He is identified as [the Christ](../christ/christ.draft.md), the promised
  Messiah and Savior.
* The Father personally and publicly declared his love for him. God called him
  "my beloved Son" (Matthew 3:17, 17:5; Mark 9:7).
* He is expected to return with [his Father](https://ofgod.info) to judge the
  world (Matthew 16:27-28; Revelation 19:11-16, 22:1-5).
* [He receives glory from God](../son-as-god/claims/glory.draft.md)
  (John 8:54, 13:32, 17:1, 5).
* [God raised him from the dead](../essence/as-god/jesus-was-resurrected.draft.md)
  (Acts 2:24,32, 17:30-31; 1 Corinthians 15:15).
* He remains active in the lives of [his disciples](../nature.md#jesus-inheritance).
  For example, he intervened during the prosecution led by Saul (Acts 9:1-6).
* He committed no sin (1 Peter 2:22; Hebrews 4:15; 1 John 3:5).
* He serves as an advocate with the Father for the forgiveness of sins
  (1 John 2:1).

## The Work of the Son

The ministry of Jesus was marked by significant actions that impacted his
followers and the world.

* He possessed the authority to raise people from the dead (John 5:21).
* He personally taught the apostle Paul (Galatians 1:11-23).
* [He sacrificed himself](../essence/of-man/jesus-was-a-blood-sacrifice.draft.md)
  for others (Ephesians 5:2).
* He freed believers from their sins through his blood (Revelation 1:5).
* He established a kingdom (Revelation 1:6).
* He made believers priests to serve his God and Father (Revelation 1:6).

## Obedience and Service to God

The actions of Jesus were driven by his relationship with God and his love for
humanity.

* [He lived in obedience to God](../essence/not-god/jesus-serve-god.draft.md)
  (John 4:34, 5:19, 6:38, 8:55, 10:37, 14:31; Acts 3:13; 1 John 4:14).
* [He demonstrated great love](../nature.md) for his followers (John 13:34-35,
  15:9; Ephesians 5:2).

## Conclusion

The Son of God is presented in scripture as a unique being who bridges the gap
between the divine and the human. He holds
[exalted titles](#biblical-titles-and-names) that reflect his authority and
relationship with the Father. While he experienced the full range of
[human emotions and challenges](#humanity-and-emotions), he also possessed
[extraordinary characteristics](#divine-nature-and-special-characteristics)
that set him apart. His [miraculous works](#the-work-of-the-son) and
[selfless service](#obedience-and-service-to-god) fulfill a divine purpose
that continues to resonate today.
