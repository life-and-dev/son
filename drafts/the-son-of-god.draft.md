# Identity

He is known as:

* "[Wonderful Counselor](what-is-jesus/as-god/prophecies/isaiah)" (Isaiah 9:6)
* "[Might One](what-is-jesus/as-god/prophecies/isaiah)" (Isaiah 9:6)
* "[Abi-Ad](what-is-jesus/as-god/prophecies/isaiah)" (Isaiah 9:6) which potentially mean "[Yahweh](/god/father/name) is my everlasting [Father](/god/father)"
* "[Prince of Peace](what-is-jesus/as-god/prophecies/isaiah)" (Isaiah 9:6)
* "The Vine" (Zechariah 3:10; John 15:1-9)
* "[Immanuel](what-is-jesus/as-god/prophecies/isaiah)" (Matthew 1:23, Isaiah 7:14) which means "[God](/god) with us"
* "Jesus" (Mark 1:1)
* "[Holy One](/god/holy)" (Mark 1:24; Luke 1:35, 4:34; John 6:69; Acts 2:27, 13:35 quoting Psalm 16:10)
* "[The Light](/bible/metaphors/light)" (John 1:5, 3:19)
* "The Lamb of God" (John 1:29,36; 1 Corinthians 5:7; 1 Peter 1:19)
* "[The Bread](/bible/metaphors/bread)" (John 6:35, John 6:48-51)
* "The Door / Gate of the sheep" (John 10:7)
* "The Good Shepherd" (John 10:14)
* "The Way, and the Truth, and the Life" (John 10:25; John 14:6; Acts 3:15)
* "[The Christ](christ)" (Acts 2:36)
* "[The Firstborn](firstborn)" (Romans 8:29; Colossians 1:15,18; Hebrews 1:6; Revelation 1:5)
* "[The Cornerstone](what-is-jesus/as-god/rock)" (Ephesians 2:20; Zechariah 3:9; 1 Corinthians 10:4)
* "[King of kings, Lord of lords](/god/son/essence/as-god/lord#king-of-kings)" (1 Timothy 6:15; Revelation 1:5, 17:14, 19:16), however Nebuchadnezzar (Daniel 2:37) and Artaxerxes (Ezra 7:12) also held this titles
* "The Faithful Witness" (Revelation 1:5)
* "The First and Last" (Revelation 1:8; Revelation 1:17)
* "The Lion of Judah" (Revelation 5:5)

# Attributes

## Jesus as a human

* He has emotions (Psalm 2:12)
* He was not a very popular person (Isaiah 53:2-3)
  * He was called a glutton and winebibber, friend of tax-collectors and sinners (Matthew 11:9)
  * His own brothers did not believe in him and mocked him (John 7:1-9)
  * He was judged by his appearance (John 7:24)
    * He did not wear soft/fine/rich-man clothes (Matthew 11:8)
* He was known as a prophet (Matthew 11:9; Luke 1:76-79)
* He did not keep all the Pharisees' laws (Matthew 12:1-2)
* He has a soul (Matthew 26:38; Mark 14:34)
* He has his own [spirit](spirit) (Matthew 27:50; Mark 2:8; Mark 8:11-12; John 11:33, 13:21, 19:30)
* [He is hu"man"](/god/son/essence/of-man/human) (Mark 15:39; John 8:40; Acts 2:22; Romans 5:15; 1 Timothy 2:5; Hebrews 10:12; Acts 17:30-31)
* He was anointed by God with [the Holy Spirit](/god/spirit) and with power (Acts 10:38)
* [He was made in the image of God](/god/son/essence/as-god/incarnation#jesus-is-the-image-of-god) (Colossians 1:15)

## What makes him special

* [Many prophets prophesied about him](prophecies)
* [He is the Father's Son](https://son.ofgod.info)
* [He had a special purpose](/god/son/purpose)
* He is not from this world (John 8:23-24)
* He is [the Christ](/bible/concepts/christ) (Messiah / Saviour)
* The Father personally and publicly declared his love to him as His "beloved Son" (Matthew 3:17; 17:5; Mark 9:7)
* He will return with [his Father](/god/father) to judge the world (Matthew 16:27-28; Revelation 19:11-16, 22:1-5)
* [He is glorified by God](/god/son/essence/as-god/claims/glory) (John 8:55, 13:32, 17:1, 17:5)
* [He was raised from the dead by God](/god/son/essence/as-god/resurrected) (Acts 2:24,32; 17:30-31; 1 Corinthians 15:15)
* He is still actively involved in [his "body" of disciples](/kingdom/body) (e.g. interfered with Saul's prosecution Acts 9:1-6)
* He committed no [sin](/bible/concepts/sin) (1 Peter 2:22; Hebrews 4:15; 1 John 3:5)
* He is our advocate with the Father to the forgiveness of [sin](/bible/concepts/sin) (1 John 2:1)

# What Jesus did

* Raise people from the dead (John 5:21)
* Taught Paul personally (Galatians 1:11-23)
* [He sacrificed himself](/bible/concepts/sacrifice) (Ephesians 5:2)
* Freed believers from their sin by his blood (Revelation 1:5)
* Established a kingdom (Revelation 1:6)
* Made believers priests to his God and Father (Revelation 1:6)
