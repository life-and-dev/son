> Whoever receives you receives me, and **whoever receives me receives Him who sent me**. -- Matthew 10:40 (ESV); Mark 9:37; Luke 9:48; John 12:44

The reasoning is that Jesus is God, therefore when you receive Jesus, you receive God, however this reasoning is flawed.

1. The first part of the same sentence is ignored: "Whoever receives **you** receives me" which, with this reasoning, should mean that you are also Jesus and God.
2. Jesus was not giving a science lesson. He was talking to his disciples and referred to their [unity with his Father](/god/son/essence/as-god/claims/one-with-father).
