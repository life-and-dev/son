> When the Son of Man comes in his glory, and all the angels with him, then he will sit on his glorious throne. -- Matthew 25:31

... but so will believers too will sit on God's throne:

> And Jesus said to them, "Truly I say to you, that you who have followed me, in the New Beginning, when the Son of Man sits on the throne of his glory, **you also will sit on twelve thrones**, judging the twelve tribes of Israel." -- Matthew 19:28-29 (ESV)

and

> The one who conquers *(or prevail)*, I *(Jesus)* will grant him to **sit with me** on my throne, as I *(Jesus)* also conquered and sat down with my Father on His throne. -- Revelation 3:21 (ESV)

This is also echoed in Revelation 1:6 and believers are not considered gods.

Even the 24 elders have thrones, and they too are not considered gods ...

> Around the throne were twenty-four thrones, and seated on the thrones were twenty-four elders, clothed in white garments, with golden crowns on their heads. -- Revelation 4:4 (ESV)

However, these 24 elders, and potentially also the Lamb who was among the elders (Revelation 5:6) cast their crowns before God's throne:

> And whenever the living creatures give glory and honor and thanks to Him who is seated on the throne, Who lives forever and ever, **the twenty-four elders fall down before Him who is seated on the throne and worship Him** who lives forever and ever. **They cast their crowns** before the throne, saying,
>
> “Worthy are you, our Lord and God, to receive glory and honor and power...” -- Revelation 4:9-11
