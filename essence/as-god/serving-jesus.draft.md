> And Jesus answered him, “It is written, “‘You shall [worship](/god/worship) the Lord your God, and **Him only shall you serve**.’” -- Luke 4:8 (ESV); Matthew 4:9-10

And yet, Jesus said:

> “**If anyone serves me**, he must follow me; and where I am, there will my servant be also. **If anyone serves me**, the Father will honor him.” -- John 12:26 (ESV)

And Paul wrote:

> Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord and not for men, knowing that from the Lord you will receive the inheritance as your reward. **You are serving the Lord Christ.** -- Colossians 3:23-24 (ESV)

Some would quote this to proof that Jesus is "the Lord your God", however this [confusing translation](/bible/concepts/serve) was caused by bible translators who [translated different Greek words with different meanings to the same English word "serve"](/bible/concepts/serve).
