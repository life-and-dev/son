> I have set you an example that **you should do** as I have done for you. -- John 13:15 (NIV)

and

> For to this you were called, because Christ also suffered for us, leaving us an example, that **you should follow** in his steps. -- 1 Peter 2:21 (NIV)

and

> This is how we know that we are in union with him:
>
> the one who says he remains in union with him *(Jesus Christ)* **is obligated to walk just as he walked**. -- 1 John 2:5-6 (REV)

A Jesus God would set unfair standards for a limited mankind. Nobody ever said which should try to be more Christ-like. Instead, the words "you should" or "obligated" is used.

Paul wrote:

> For those whom He *(God)* foreknew He also predestined **to be conformed to the image of His Son**, in order that he might be the firstborn among many brothers. -- Romans 8:29 (ESV)

The Jews understood that:

> A disciple is not above his teacher, but everyone when he is fully trained **will be like his teacher**. -- Luke 6:40 (ESV)

These were Jesus' words. Jesus would not have said this if he knew it would be impossible for his human disciples to be like him.
