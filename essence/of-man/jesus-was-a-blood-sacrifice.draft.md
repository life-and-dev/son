Many [Christians](/bible/concepts/christian) do not understand [the purpose of the crucifixion](/god/son/crucifixion).

[The crucifixion](/god/son/crucifixion) required a genuine 100% real human of our kind to reconcile mankind with God, by paying as a human for the sins of mankind.

> For as **by a man came death**, **by [a man](/god/son/essence/of-man) has come also the resurrection** of the dead.  
> For as in **Adam all die**, so also in **[Christ](/god/son/christ) shall all be made alive**.
>
> -- 1 Corinthians 15:21-22 (ESV)

It were humans who that trespassed, not God. Therefore, a human life (blood) was required to pay for sin. 

Many believe that it was God that died on [the cross](/bible/symbolism/cross). Technically the Almighty God could potentially do anything, but that would either mean that He had to trick us, by pretending to suffer and die, or violate His own commands and principles. Neither are acceptable to [God's character](/god/father).

Despite that we rebelled, God still demonstrated His love by making a "sacrifice" for us. 

By definition, a "sacrifice" means to give up something valuable. However, God is the Creator. What could He possibly give up that He simply could not just be recreated afterward which would disqualify the concept of a sacrifice?

Therefore, God has up His only-begotten son in whom He was well pleased, who were distinct, who had a freewill, who voluntarily choose to obey Him until death, who God loved dearly. It must have been very hard for God to see His son suffer and to forsake him.

By doing this, God established a [new covenant](/god/covenants/christ) with His son so that we too can live in [righteousness](/bible/concepts/righteousness) with Him. This was not some clever new idea of Jesus. [It was prophesied millennia ago by various prophets.](/god/son/essence/of-man/prophecies)

Therefore, [Jesus is our way, truth and life](/god/son/purpose) to [God, his Father](/god/father).
