# Spirit of the Antichrist

John wrote:

> By this you know the Spirit of God:
>
> - Every spirit that confesses that **Jesus Christ has come in the flesh** is of God, and
> - every spirit that does not confess that **Jesus Christ has come in the flesh** is not of God. And this is the spirit of **the Antichrist**, which you have heard was coming, and is now already in the world.
>
> — 1 John 4:2-3 (NKJV)

Out of context this seems to imply that everyone who deny the incarnation of Jesus Christ has the spirit of the Antichrist.

## Context

The letter of John should not have been divided up in chapters. Unfortuantely I cannot quote the entire leter here, so I will highlight only a few important points.

In John's life-time there was an ancient belief that [Christ was divine](../../divine.md) but *not genuinely human*, called **Docetism**. The term comes from the Greek word dokeō (δοκέω), meaning “to seem” or “to appear.” Docetism taught that *Jesus only appeared to have a physical human body, suffer, and die*, rather than experiencing these things as a genuine human being. This belief appeared in various forms in early Christianity, particularly during the second century, although related ideas may have existed earlier. This is evident from scriptures like:

> What was from the beginning, what **we have heard**, what **we have seen** with our eyes, what **we beheld and touched** with our hands, concerning [the Word of Life](../../name/word.md)—and **the life was manifested**, and we have seen and bear witness and proclaim to you [the eternal life](https://kingdom.ofgod.info/life), which was with the Father and was manifested to us— what we have **seen** and **heard** we proclaim to you also, so that you may also have fellowship with us; and indeed our fellowship is with the Father, and with His Son Jesus Christ. — 1 John 1:1-3 (LSB)

A similiar narrative like John 1 is presented here. God's Word of Life "manifested" which means God kept His word (promise). Note how John repeatedly mention that they physically heard, seen and touched Jesus Christ. John want to make sure his audience clearly understood that Jesus was [genuinely human](../../human.md).

## Defining "Antichrist"

Many churches claims that denying *[the divinity of Jesus](https://son.ofgod.info/divine)* is the antichrist, however according to John it is the opposite.

"[The Christ](https://kingdom.ofgod.info/christ)" means "the anointed one", which implies a greater authority had to anoint Jesus. John defines the "antichrist" as follows:

> Who is the liar but the one who **denies that Jesus is the Christ**?
>
> **This is the antichrist, the one who denies the Father and the Son**.
>
> - Everyone who denies the Son does not have the Father;
> - the one who confesses the Son has the Father also.
>
> As for you, let that which you heard from the beginning **abide in you**. If what you heard from the beginning **abides in you**, you also will **abide in the Son and in the Father**.
>
> And this is the promise which He Himself made to us: **eternal life**.
>
> — 1 John 2:22-25 (LSB)

John specified that "denies the Son" means "denies that Jesus is the Christ" which means to deny that Jesus is the anointed one of God. This is not just a matter of philosophy. It affects "eternal life"!

> [!TIP]
> As John stated, "abide in the Son/Father" language means to hear (obey). This is how one test if someone has the Spirit of God or the Spirit of the antichrist.

Many churches claims that denying *[the divinity of Jesus](https://son.ofgod.info/divine)* is the antichrist, however according to John it is the opposite. **Denying that Jesus was anointed by God is the anitchrist.** To claim that Jesus was not anointed because he is God Himself would rather be "antichrist".

Now with this context read 1 John 3 & 4 again:

> ... And **by this we will know that we are of the truth**, and will **assure our heart before Him** *[the Father - 1 John 3:1]* in whatever **our heart condemns us**; for God is greater than our heart and knows all things. Beloved, **if our heart does not condemn us**, we have confidence before God; and whatever we ask we receive from Him, because we keep His commandments and do the things that are pleasing in His sight.
>
> And **this is His commandment**, that **we [believe](https://word.ofgod.info/terms/faith) in [the name of His Son Jesus Christ](../../name.md)**, and love one another, just as **He gave a commandment to us**. And **the one who keeps His commandments abides in Him, and He in him**. **We know by this that He abides in us, by the Spirit whom He gave us**.  Beloved, do not believe every spirit, but test the spirits to see whether they are from God, because many false prophets have gone out into the world. **By this you know the Spirit of God**:
>
> - every spirit that confesses that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is from God, and
> - every spirit that does not confess Jesus is not from God. This is the *spirit* of the antichrist, of which you have heard that it is coming, and now it is already in the world.
>
> You are from God, little children, and have overcome them; because **greater is He who is in you** than he who is in the world.
>
> - They are from the world: therefore they speak *as* from the world, and the world hears them.

>

>
> — 1 John 3:19 - 4:6 (LSB)

In plain English, according to John, this is how "we will know that we are of the truth" (having the Spirit of God):

### Personal Test

When someone with God's Spirit hear God's commandments (given by Christ) the test come with their free will choice:

- When they obey: Then God's Spirit will "assure" them
- When they disobey: Then God's Spirit will "condemn" them

This is because God's Spirit knows all things and we can use this as evidence that we "abide in Him" (obeyed Him) in [righteousness](https://kingdom.ofgod.info/life/righteousness).

### Testing Another Party

Other persons could be tested by their confession:

- Those who confess the Son (Jesus Christ) has the Father also.
- Those who denies the son (Jesus Christ) denies the Father also.

This is because [the Son is the Father's representitive](../agency.md).

## Salvation

At the end of the book John warns about deceptions and explain what we should belief to be saved:

> Everyone who believes that **Jesus is the [Christ]** has been born of God, and everyone who loves the One who gives new birth loves also the one who has been born of Him. By this we know that we love the children of God, **when we love God** and **do His commandments**. For this is the love of God, that **we keep His commandments**; and **His commandments** are not burdensome. For everything that has been born of God overcomes the world; and this is the overcoming that has overcome the world — **our [faith](https://word.ofgod.info/terms/faith).**
>
> Who is the one who overcomes the world, but **he who believes that Jesus is the Son of God**?
>
> This is **the One who came by water and blood, Jesus Christ; not with the water only, but with the water and with the blood**. It is **the Spirit who bears witness**, because the Spirit is the truth. For there are three that bear witness:
>
> 1. the Spirit and
> 2. the water and
> 3. the blood;
>
> and the three are in agreement.
>
> — 1 John 5:1-8 (LSB)

With this passage John made it clear that Jesus Christ was really born as like any ordinary human being in water and blood. Then he highlights that the Spirit witness in truth that he is ["the Son of God"](https://son.ofgod.info) and not God Himself.

> [!NOTE]
> Some associate this scripture with repentance, baptism and receiving the Holy Spirit, but in this context it more likely was used as plain evidence that Jesus was a [genuinely human](https://son.ofgod.info/human).

> [!CAUTION]
> The Roman Catholic church had to carefully added 1 John 5:7-8, to the TR (Textus Receptive), because this passage [refutes the Trinity Doctrine](https://son.ofgod.info/trinity) of 3 divine persons of which Jesus is supposed to be one of them. If John testifies that Jesus was a naturally born human, born in water and blood, then the Trinity fails because it requires *[Jesus to be divine](https://son.ofgod.info/divine)*.
>
> The added verses changes John's testimony 5:7-8 to *"in heaven, the Father, the Word, and the Holy Spirit; and these three are one"*
>
>> This later addition is known as the Comma Johanneum, one of the most famous textual variants in the New Testament. Although present in the Latin Vulgate, this expanded text is not found as the base text in any Greek manuscript before the 12th century. — [AICNT](https://corebible.app/1JN/5?verseNumber=%2220%22&translation=AICNT)
>
> Therefore, any bible like the KJV and NKJV which were translated from the Vulgate will include these errors too.

In the context of 1 John 4:2-3, an obvious test for deception would be to check if they confess that "Christ come in flesh" (born like any other human). John was not affirming [concious preexistance](../../divine/preexistance.md). Instead John was saying that **those follow Docetism** (rejecting that Jesus was a genuine human) are being deceived by the spirit of the **antichrist**.

Today we have a similiar situation. Like the Docetians, the [Trinitarians](../../trinity.md) belief Jesus Christ was [divine](../../divine.md), although they also acknowledge his a *simultaneous [human nature](../../dual-nature.md)*. This makes them guilty of claiming that Jesus Christ was *not a genuine human*, because no other genuine human has a divine and human nature simultaneously.

John even concludes his book with the warning:

> Little children, **guard yourselves from idols**. — 1 John 5:21 (LSB)
