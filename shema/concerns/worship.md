# Could Refusing Jesus Worship Be Idolatry?

If Jesus is God, refusing him worship would be a grave mistake. If he is not God, giving him the Father’s exclusive worship would also be a grave mistake. The word *worship* can describe two different acts: honouring a king and offering sacrifice or sacred service. The distinction matters.

## The Idolatry Concern

God forbids allegiance to other gods (Exodus 20:3–5). Paul identifies “**one God, the Father**” and “**one Lord, Jesus Christ**” (1 Corinthians 8:6). Honouring Jesus as the Father’s appointed Lord is not allegiance to another god. Nor does refusing to offer Jesus sacrifice mean refusing him honour.

## Honour Jesus as Lord

The Father entrusted judgment to Jesus “**that all may honor the Son, just as they honor the Father**” (John 5:22–23). God made Jesus Lord and Christ (Acts 2:36); every knee bows and every tongue confesses him as Lord “**to the glory of God the Father**” (Philippians 2:9–11). People also bowed before the risen Jesus (Matthew 28:9, 17). This [worship as paying obeisance](../../divine/worship.md#paying-obeisance-worship) (honour, praise, bowing, and confession) is permitted. Refusing Jesus that honour would dishonour the Father Who [sent](../../human/serve-god.md) him.

## Sacrificial Worship Belongs to the Father

[Sacrificial worship](../../divine/worship.md#sacrificial-worship) is different: it includes offerings, sacred service, and dedicating one’s life to the recipient. Jesus never asked for sacrificial worship. He taught that God alone is to be served (Matthew 4:10). Believers present their bodies to **God** (Romans 12:1) and offer spiritual sacrifices **to God through Jesus Christ** (1 Peter 2:5). Offering to God *through* Jesus is not offering to Jesus. Sacrificial worship offered to Jesus is forbidden by [God’s exclusive claim to that service](../../divine/worship.md#only-yhwh-may-be-served-by-sacrificial-worship).

## Conclusion

[Honouring Jesus as Lord](#honour-jesus-as-lord) does not violate [God’s exclusive claim](#sacrificial-worship-belongs-to-the-father). Praise, bow to, and confess Jesus as God’s appointed king.
