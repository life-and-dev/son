# Did Jesus’ Miracles Prove He Is God?

Jesus’ signs reveal God’s kingdom and Jesus’ unique authority. [God Worked Through Jesus](#god-worked-through-jesus) weighs the strongest Trinitarian and Unitarian readings.

## God Worked Through Jesus

Acts names Jesus “a man attested by God,” not a rival source of God’s power. Jesus’ signs confirm God’s presence and authenticate his mission.

Trinitarians can respond that Jesus performs signs by his own word and authority. That observation is valid. Yet John records Jesus saying that the Father dwelling in him does the works, and Acts attributes the miracles to God working through him (John 14:10; Acts 2:22).

*God’s action through Jesus establishes a unique agency relationship, not identity by itself.*
