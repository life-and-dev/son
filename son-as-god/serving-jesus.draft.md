# Does Serving Jesus Make Him God?

Christians serve Jesus as risen Lord. [Service and Worship in Context](#service-and-worship-in-context) considers devotion without word-root fallacies.

## Service and Worship in Context

The Greek terms must not be made to carry conclusions from their roots. *Latreuō* commonly denotes sacred service, *douleuō* ordinary or religious service, and *proskyneō* ranges from homage before humans to worship of God. Context identifies the act and recipient.

The Father commands that all honour the Son, and every knee will bow to Jesus (John 5:23; Philippians 2:10-11). Trinitarians see this as worship owed to God. Unitarians see God commanding honour to His appointed Lord, with the final confession directed to the Father’s glory.

*The evidence requires profound devotion to Jesus, but lexical range alone does not prove he is God.*
