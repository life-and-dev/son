## Conclusion

The similarities are real and should be neither inflated nor dismissed. The [Philippians 2 comparison article](../similarities.md#philippians-26) provides further context for the passage; [Jesus as Lord](../lord.draft.md#philippians-29-11) considers its Isaiah allusion.

*The cumulative Unitarian reading recognises Jesus’ unique, God-given likeness and authority while maintaining the Father as the one God.*
