## The One God and His Son

Jesus is distinct from the one God in apostolic confession, while uniquely exalted as God’s Messiah.

> “For us there is **one God, the Father** ... and **one Lord, Jesus Christ**.” — 1 Corinthians 8:6 (ESV)

Trinitarians assemble divine-identity, creation, devotion, two-natures, and resurrection-authority texts. Unitarians give those texts weight while observing that God sends, empowers, raises, and exalts Jesus. *The Unitarian conclusion is that the Father alone is the one God and Jesus is His saving Messiah.*
