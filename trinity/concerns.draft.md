# Concerns from Trinitarians

For some Christians, questioning the Trinity feels dangerous rather than merely difficult. They fear dishonouring God, losing salvation, or making Jesus' death seem small.

The question is whether Scripture requires a person to call Jesus Almighty God and the Holy Spirit a separate divine person to proclaim faith.

## Why These Concerns Feel Serious

Trinitarians affirm Jesus' humanity, distinguish the Father, Son and Holy Spirit, and profess belief in one God, not three. *A Biblical Unitarian reading cannot establish its whole account of Jesus merely by distinguishing him from the Father.* These questions deserve attention to what Scripture says about his honour, salvation and the Spirit.

- **[Blasphemy](../concerns/blasphemy.draft.md)**: If Jesus is God, would saying he is human but not Almighty God insult him?
- **[The unforgivable sin](../concerns/unforgivable-sin.draft.md)**: If someone calls the Holy Spirit a force instead of God, could that be blasphemy against the Holy Spirit?
- **[Salvation](../concerns/salvation.draft.md)**: If believing in Jesus requires believing he is Almighty God, could denying that cost someone salvation?
- **[The cross](../concerns/the-cross.draft.md)**: Can one man's death save billions of people, or must the sacrifice be divine to be sufficient?
- **[Worship](../concerns/worship.draft.md)**: If Jesus is God, could refusing him worship and honouring another lord become idolatry?
- **[Fellowship](../concerns/fellowship.draft.md)**: If Jesus remains a human person, how can he be close to believers across the world?
- **[Known by Jesus](../concerns/known-by-jesus.draft.md)**: Jesus warns in Matthew 7:21–23 that he never knew some who called him Lord. If Jesus is human, how can believers communicate with him and be known by him across the world?
- **[God's love](../concerns/gods-love.draft.md)**: Would it be cruel for the Father to send His Son to die rather than die Himself?
- **[Tradition](../concerns/tradition.draft.md)**: If so many Christians have confessed Jesus as Almighty God for centuries, how could they all be mistaken? Why was this not resolved during the Reformation?

The questions lead to related passages and practical costs. Thomas's confession and the Word are considered among [the strongest worship texts](../concerns/worship.draft.md#the-strongest-worship-texts-need-hearing). The discussions of the Spirit address [personal activity](../concerns/unforgivable-sin.draft.md#the-spirit-is-not-an-impersonal-force) and [the Spirit the Father gives](../concerns/unforgivable-sin.draft.md#the-spirit-the-father-gives). [Tradition](../concerns/tradition.draft.md#when-examination-costs-relationships) considers the cost of examination within families and churches, alongside Israel and the Reformation.

The [human Messiah's obedience and glory](../concerns/the-cross.draft.md#why-a-human-messiah-matters) and [the Father's initiating love](../concerns/gods-love.draft.md#the-father-initiates-reconciliation) matter to the discussion of the cross. The Father knows and reaches his people through [the fellowship he gives](../concerns/fellowship.draft.md#the-father-knows-and-reaches-his-people). Questions about relationship with Jesus, apostolic witness and communication are addressed under [knowing and being known](../concerns/known-by-jesus.draft.md#knowing-and-being-known-by-jesus) and [whether a human Jesus can hear us](../concerns/known-by-jesus.draft.md#can-a-human-jesus-hear-us). *The mechanism need not be claimed where Scripture leaves it unexplained.*

## Conclusion

*On this reading, the Father alone is Almighty God. He establishes the new covenant through his willing human Son's sacrifice, grants forgiveness and reconciliation, and raises Jesus to renewed life. Jesus receives extraordinary honour and glory without replacing the Father; the Spirit is the Father's active presence, not an inert force.* The value of Jesus' sacrifice is not a calculation of how much blood could save how many people.

Matthew 7:21–23 addresses doing the Father's will and the danger of lawlessness; Jesus' following words call hearers to act on what he says. *The warning does not prescribe a Nicene confession or prove that a person must hear Jesus' private voice. Nor does either doctrinal label guarantee salvation.* The response explored in [salvation](../concerns/salvation.draft.md#how-to-respond) and [fellowship](../concerns/fellowship.draft.md#grace-makes-disciples-active) is faith expressed through active discipleship, not a quota of works.
